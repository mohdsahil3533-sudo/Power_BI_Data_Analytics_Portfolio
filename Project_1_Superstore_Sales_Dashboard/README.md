# 📊 Super Store Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Super Store Sales Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, regional sales, and shipping methods.

The dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful visual insights, helping businesses monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify trends, and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, profit, and order quantity.
- Understand sales performance across different regions.
- Identify the most profitable and best-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- Analyze customer segments and their contribution to sales.
- Evaluate shipping methods and average delivery time.
- Track monthly sales and profit trends over time.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Measures and KPI calculations
- **Data Modeling** – Organizing data relationships
- **Data Visualization** – Interactive charts, cards, and slicers

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard displays the following metrics:

- 💰 **Total Sales:** 1.6M
- 📦 **Total Order Quantity:** 22K
- 💵 **Total Profit:** 175K
- 🚚 **Average Delivery Time:** 4 days

*Note: These figures are displayed as rounded values in the dashboard.*

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales by Segment
Analyzes sales contribution from three customer segments:
- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

### 2. Sales by Region
Visualizes sales distribution across:
- Central
- East
- South
- West

The dashboard also includes regional filtering for focused analysis.

### 3. Sales by Category
Compares sales across the major product categories:
- Office Supplies
- Technology
- Furniture

### 4. Sales by Sub-Category
Highlights leading sub-categories, including:
- Phones
- Chairs
- Binders

### 5. Sales by Ship Mode
Analyzes sales across different shipping methods:
- Standard Class
- Second Class
- First Class
- Same Day

### 6. Monthly Sales and Profit Trends
Uses time-series visualizations to explore monthly sales and profit fluctuations over the displayed period.

### 7. Interactive Regional Filters
Allows users to filter dashboard data by region for more focused comparisons and analysis.

## 💡 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard visualizations:

- **Consumer** is the largest customer segment, contributing approximately 48% of sales.
- **West** is the leading sales region, contributing approximately 33%.
- **Office Supplies** is the highest-selling category, with sales of around 0.6M.
- **Phones** is the leading displayed sub-category, with sales of approximately 196.6K.
- **Standard Class** accounts for the largest sales volume among the displayed shipping methods.
- Monthly sales and profit show fluctuations over time, making trend monitoring important for business planning.
